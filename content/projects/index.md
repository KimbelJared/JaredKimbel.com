## Data Automation & Workflow Engineering
I build structured, reliable workflows that improve financial data integrity, reduce manual effort, and increase reporting confidence. My work focuses on reconciliation, ERP reporting, and operational finance processes — applying a systems mindset to ensure accuracy at scale.

### Automated Revenue Reporting Workflow (Allegiant Airlines)
**Problem:** Manual daily reporting workflows created bottlenecks and increased risk of inconsistencies across datasets.  
**Approach:** Designed an automated reporting workflow that standardized inputs, validated data integrity, and produced consistent daily outputs for revenue operations.  
**Impact:** Reduced manual processing time by ~25% while improving reporting reliability.  
**Tools:** SQL, Power Query, PowerShell, Excel

## Independent Systems Architecture (Technical Hobby)
Outside of work, I build dabble in game development to sharpen structured thinking and modular design. I use milestone-driven planning, and data-driven design to keep complex projects organized and scalable.

### Modular State Architecture for Complex Systems
**Problem:** Larger projects become brittle without clear boundaries between states, behaviors, and data.  
**Approach:** Designed modular state-based architecture to isolate responsibilities, reduce coupling, and support iterative expansion (e.g., adding new abilities or behaviors without rewriting core logic).  
**Impact:** Improved maintainability and development velocity by enabling reusable patterns and predictable system behavior.  
**Focus Areas:** modular design, separation of concerns, extensible architecture

---

### Data-Driven Taxonomy Design (Skills / Items / Progression)
**Problem:** Scaling content requires consistency in naming, categorization, and progression logic.  
**Approach:** Built structured taxonomies for skills, tiers, and progression rules with data-driven definitions to minimize hard-coded behavior.  
**Impact:** Enabled easier balancing and expansion while keeping systems coherent as scope increased.  
**Focus Areas:** schema design, consistency, scalability

---

### Milestone-Based Planning & Iteration for Long-Running Builds
**Problem:** Large projects stall without clear milestones, scope control, and iteration loops.  
**Approach:** Planned work in milestones with explicit deliverables, test checkpoints, and refinement cycles—similar to phased rollout planning in business systems.  
**Impact:** Reduced rework and kept development aligned to measurable outcomes over time.
